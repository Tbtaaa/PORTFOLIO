Callao
Metropolitan Development Plan

Urban Planning Data Communication

The Callao Metropolitan Development Plan defines a long-term vision for the spatial development of Metropolitan Callao towards 2040. The plan considers how the metropolitan area can be structured around its existing and emerging centralities, urban facilities, mobility networks and different patterns of development.

Alongside the technical work, I produced maps, diagrams and 3D visualisations used to explain proposals and support discussions with non-technical audiences. I also participated in workshops with stakeholders and local communities, where these materials helped communicate and discuss the future vision for Callao.
