Population density growth
and mass transport infrastructure

Urban Data Science Transport

This project investigates whether proximity to mass-transit infrastructure is associated with population density growth in Lima and Callao between 2007 and 2017. Using neighbourhood-scale hexagonal units, the analysis combines census, transport, topographic and socioeconomic data to examine patterns of urban densification and test whether areas near Metro Line 1 and the Metropolitano BRT experienced greater population growth.

Methodology

Hexagonal spatial analysis · Areal interpolation · Proximity analysis · Slope analysis · Linear regression · Multivariate regression · Regression diagnostics

Tools

R · RStudio · GIS
