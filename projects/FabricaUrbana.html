The Urban Factory

Urban Design Urban Planning

The Urban Factory explores the regeneration of the industrial corridor between Lima and Callao, rethinking an area shaped by large industrial sites, fragmented urban fabrics and major transport infrastructure.

Developed as my architecture thesis, the project investigated how the transformation of former and active industrial land could create new connections between neighbourhoods and introduce a more diverse mix of urban activities. The proposal combined an urban-scale regeneration strategy with the detailed development of selected intervention areas, using public space, new programmes and improved connectivity to reconnect previously separated parts of the city.
