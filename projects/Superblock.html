Superblock Surco

Urban Design Data Communication

Superblock Surco explores how the historic centre of Surco could be transformed into a safer and more pedestrian-oriented neighbourhood by reorganising traffic and reclaiming street space for people. The project was developed around a superblock approach, combining changes in circulation with new public spaces and improved conditions for walking and everyday street life.

A key part of the process was the use of tactical urbanism to test proposed changes through temporary, small-scale interventions before translating them into a permanent design. The project was developed through participatory workshops, where residents contributed to the discussion and development of proposals, allowing local perspectives to inform the design process.

I contributed to these workshops, the development of the tactical interventions and the final urban design proposal, as well as preparing visual material to communicate the project and present the proposals during public meetings with local government representatives and residents.
