Urban Development Plan

Urban Planning Data Communication

The Lima–Callao Urban Mobility Plan defines the future development of the metropolitan transport system, bringing together proposals for metro, BRT, cable car and other mobility infrastructure.

I worked as part of the planning team, organising and analysing geospatial data and producing technical maps, 3D models and visualisations to explore proposals and communicate them clearly within a multidisciplinary team and to non-technical audiences.

My work also included the development of urban and transport planning methodologies and spatial materials used throughout the planning process.
